In this THM Room, we can learn how to geolocate images. [^1] 

Image 1 - Geolocating Images 101
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
On first sight, this image looks like Chicago's "Bean" but the reverse image
search with Yandex reveals it is a Chinese Knockoff called the "Big Oil Bubble".

Image 2 - Now your turn
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We head over to Google Maps and search for the "Sheffield Avenue". This would however not
be sufficient, and we instead search for the "Sheffield Avenue and Addison Street" to 
search for the significant crossroad from the picture. Google Maps then finds this [^2]
place in Chicago that matches the "Sports Corner" sign and street alignment. Based of the
Google Street View [^3], the shop "Wrigleyville Sports" in the corner of the intersection
has the exact view as seen in the picture.

Image 3 - Your turn, again!
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the background there is a large big tower that looks like the Eiffel Tower and in the
foreground there a buildings that look like they could belong to an observatory. And
indeed, by turning on Satellite Imagery for the region in Paris that might come into mind
for this view, we find the "Coupole de la Table Équatoriale" that matches the building
formation and skyline of Paris perfectly. The name of the observatory this table dome
formation belongs to is the "Meudon Observatory" in Paris. [^4]

Image 4 - Your turn, what can you see?
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Firstly, one can see that the cars drive on the left side of the street so we suspect it
to be in the United Kingdom. Further there are uniquely looking yellow light bulbs with
black and white stripes on the street and a raven sign in the middle of the street. On
the bottom right corner there are Graffiti marks and a rose with the name "Terry Jan" on
it which might indicate a memorial. The vehicle registration number "LOIO" on the front
silver car licence plate indicates the location to be in London. Finally, we reverse
search the image in Yandex and are prompted with the "Beatles Abbey Road Crossing" from
the significant album cover and explains the Graffiti to the Abbey Road Shop from Fans
of the Beatles.
